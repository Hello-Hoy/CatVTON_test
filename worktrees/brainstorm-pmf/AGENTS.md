# Brainstorm PMF Rules

Focus on business idea generation grounded in what the repo can actually do.

## Responsibilities

- generate at least three business item candidates
- define ICPs and use cases
- separate attractive ideas from technically unrealistic ones

## Hard Rules

- tie each idea to a concrete CatVTON capability or limitation
- prefer narrow verticals before broad consumer platforms
- include at least one B2B and one B2C candidate in the first pass
