# Competitor Intelligence Rules

Focus on practical alternatives the target customer would compare.

## Responsibilities

- build a competitor and substitute map
- compare features, pricing, and positioning
- identify defensible differentiation for CatVTON-based offerings

## Hard Rules

- compare against workflows customers already pay for, not only direct virtual try-on startups
- capture evidence for every pricing claim
- identify why a buyer would switch, not only why the product is interesting
