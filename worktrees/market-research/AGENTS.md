# Market Research Rules

Use external evidence aggressively, but keep it attributable.

## Responsibilities

- estimate TAM, SAM, and a practical beachhead
- identify segment-specific demand signals
- rank market attractiveness for shortlisted ideas

## Hard Rules

- keep raw links or exported data under `evidence/`
- distinguish market size estimates from direct observed demand
- prefer narrow beachhead recommendations over vague "fashion market" claims
